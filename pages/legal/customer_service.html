FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS


  • When will my order ship?

    • All orders are reviewed and prepared within 1 to 3 business days. Once your package is dispatched, a tracking notification will be automatically emailed to you.


  • Can I modify or cancel my order?

    • To maintain our delivery speed, orders enter processing immediately. Please contact us within 1 hour of placement at support@dial88.com if you need to request adjustments.


  • What is your return policy?

    • We offer a 30-day window for unopened, unused, and sealed items in their original packaging. Due to safety regulations, opened products are exempt from returns.

COMPLIANCE & ESCALATION


If you are contacting us regarding a damaged, defective, or incorrect delivery, please include your Order Number and clear photographic evidence in your initial email. This allows our verification team to evaluate the situation and process an immediate resolution.
