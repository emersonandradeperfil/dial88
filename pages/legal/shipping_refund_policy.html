SHIPPING & REFUND POLICY

Last Updated: October 31, 2026

Thank you for choosing dial88®. We are dedicated to delivering our curated portfolio of premium products safely and efficiently to your doorstep. Please review our shipping, delivery, and refund procedures below.

SHIPPING & DELIVERY FRAMEWORKS


* Processing Time: All orders are reviewed, prepared, and dispatched within 1 to 3 business days (excluding weekends and holidays) after receiving your order confirmation email. You will receive another notification when your order has shipped.
* Shipping Rates: Shipping charges and options for your order will be calculated and clearly displayed at checkout before payment completion.
* Tracking Orders: Once your package is dispatched, you will receive an email containing a unique tracking number to monitor your delivery status. Please allow up to 48 hours for the tracking information to become active.
* International Shipping: For orders shipped internationally, deliveries may be subject to local import duties, taxes, or customs clearance procedures. These dynamic fees are the sole responsibility of the consumer.

REFUNDS, RETURNS, AND BRAND POLICIES


To maintain the absolute purity and compliance standards of our curated items, we accept returns and issue refunds based on strict safety guidelines:

* Return Window: We offer a 30-day return window from the date of product delivery. To be eligible for a return, your item must be unused, unopened, in its original packaging, and in the exact condition that you received it.
* Exempt Items: Due to safety regulations and quality control frameworks, any item that has been opened, unsealed, or altered from its original packaging cannot be accepted for return or refund.
* How to Initiate a Return: To start a return process, please contact our customer care team at support@dial88.com with your order number and proof of purchase. If your return is accepted, we will provide you with detailed instructions on where and how to send your package.

DAMAGES, DEFECTS, AND MISPLACED ITEMS


We take rigorous care in our dispatch fulfillment. However, if you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, please inspect your order immediately upon receipt and contact us within 7 days of delivery.

Please include your order number and clear photographic evidence of the issue at support@dial88.com so we can evaluate the situation and correct it immediately.

PROCESSING REFUNDS


Once we receive and inspect your returned item, we will notify you regarding the approval or rejection of your refund. If approved, your refund will be processed automatically, and a credit will be applied directly to your original method of payment within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your financial institution's processing times.
